Win chance = FightIQ's blend of the stats model and the bookmakers' odds where historical odds exist (2015 on), otherwise the stats model alone. Hit rate = share of a card's fights (with a winner) where FightIQ's pick won; avg confidence = FightIQ's average win chance for its picks on that card. Bets follow the site's rule (edge over 5%, no debuts) at a flat 1 unit, exactly as in the backtest. Before 2006 there were few earlier UFC fights to learn from, so those picks are rough.

FightIQ is free and fully transparent. Every pick is published before the fight, locked at the first bout and kept in the public code repository.
