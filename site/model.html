The method

How FightIQ works

Everything is public: the method, the code, the backtest and the live results. Here is the short version, in four rounds.

Round 1

Real fight data

Round-by-round stats for every UFC bout since UFC 1, from ufcstats.com, refreshed daily, plus each fighter's pro record outside the UFC from Sherdog.

Round 2

Only what was known

Each fighter's numbers are rebuilt as they stood the day before the fight. No hindsight.

Round 3

Compare the fighters

A logistic regression (plain statistics, not AI) turns 23 differences between the two fighters into a win chance.

Round 4

Blend with the odds

The model adjusts the bookmakers' price. A bet needs a 5%+ edge; stakes are small and capped.

On its own, a stats model doesn't beat the betting market: bookmakers know a lot. FightIQ's edge comes from combining the two. It starts from the market's price and nudges it using the stats, which beat the market's own probabilities on 5,189 historical fights (log loss 0.6023 vs 0.6043).

Model trained on 8,376 UFC bouts (2005-02-05 to 2026-10-24). Both fighters are always considered from both corners, so the order they're listed in can't change a prediction. Blend: logit(p) = 0.889 × logit(bookmaker p) + 0.314 × model score.

Bet rules

  • Edge 5%+: a bet is only suggested when the best available odds beat FightIQ's win chance by 5% or more.
  • Fair odds: the bookmaker's margin is removed with the power method, which takes more of it off the longshot (bookmakers overprice longshots). This made the backtest more accurate than splitting the margin evenly.
  • Small, capped stakes: a quarter of the Kelly amount, never more than 2u (1u = 1% of bankroll).
  • No debut bets: there are no UFC stats for debutants, and the backtest showed no edge on those fights.
  • Locked at fight time: picks are final when the first bout starts and are never edited.

Honest caveats

  • The historical odds come from a public dataset that doesn't say whether they're opening or closing prices. If they're openers, the real edge is smaller than the backtest shows. The live record on this site is the real test.
  • Bets are rare (a handful per card) and fights are noisy, so even a genuine edge can lose for months.
  • The model knows nothing about injuries, weight cuts, short-notice changes or camp news.
  • An earlier version of this model claimed 74.5% accuracy; that was an error (it used fighters' future stats). Its real live record was 62%. What went wrong and how it was fixed.

Data and code

  • Fight stats: ufcstats.com, via the daily public scrape at Greco1899/scrape_ufc_stats.
  • Live odds: The Odds API, best price across Australian bookmakers.
  • Historical odds for the backtest: ultimate_ufc_dataset.
  • All code, picks and results: github.com/johnthewizwe23/FightIQ.
